COMMAND:
curl -sI https://app.team1.test:8443/api/status

OUTPUT:
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Server: nginx/1.31.6
Date: Sun, 04 Oct 2026 20:27:21 GMT
Content-Type: application/json
Connection: keep-alive
X-Backend: B
Cache-Control: public, max-age=60

EXPLANATION:
The /api/status endpoint returns Cache-Control: public, max-age=60.
This instructs clients and intermediate caches that the response may be
stored in any cache and is considered fresh for 60 seconds.

The /api/cache endpoint additionally returns an ETag header for
conditional request support (see cache-200.txt and cache-304.txt).
